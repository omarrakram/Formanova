# Buy 1 Get 1 Free weekend: strategy

**Offer:** buy any two pieces and the lower-priced one is free. It is applied automatically at checkout, every piece is eligible, and there is no code.
**Window:** Friday 20 November 2026, 7:30 PM (Cairo) → Sunday 22 November at midnight: about 52 hours.
**Goal:** the most two-piece orders in a short window, with one film for each day.

The offer terms were confirmed by the brand owner on 20 November 2026. Prices and availability come from the live storefront at **12:49 UTC on 20 November 2026**, re-confirmed at 13:07 UTC. On Saturday 21 November at 06:14 UTC one featured piece, the Italian Murano Glass Vase, had sold; film 02's second pair was replaced and the nine remaining pieces were re-recorded at unchanged prices.

---

## 1. What this offer rewards, and what the films must do

Buy 1 Get 1 on one-of-a-kind pieces is a **basket builder**: nothing is free until a second piece is in the cart, and the value of the gift depends on which two pieces the customer chooses. The films therefore have four jobs:

1. **Be understood in one second, with the sound off.** "1+1" and «اشتري قطعة… والتانية هدية» ("buy a piece… the second is a gift") open the Arabic films; "Two pieces. One price." opens the English one.
2. **Answer "which piece is free?" before anyone asks.** Every pair labels one piece *buy* and the other *gift* or *free*, and states the rule: the lower-priced piece is the gift. This removes the commonest Buy 1 Get 1 complaint at checkout.
3. **Make the gift feel valuable.** Every free piece is a real, named piece with its real price struck through. Nothing is described in the abstract.
4. **Give a true reason to act now.** The offer really ends on Sunday at midnight. Each film states the deadline in the way that is true on the day it runs.

## 3. Three films, three days

| | 01 · 1+1, Tonight | 02 · Two Pieces, One Price | 03 · Last Day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runs | Friday from 7:30 PM | Saturday (may continue on Sunday) | Sunday until midnight |
| Language | Egyptian Arabic | English | Egyptian Arabic |
| Job | Launch: teach the offer and prove it at three budgets | Persuade considered buyers with curated pairs and a dated deadline | Convert everyone who meant to buy |
| Opening frame | Gold **1+1** and «من الليلة الساعة 7:30» | *Two pieces.* ***One price.*** over two arches | **آخر يوم** over a clock at two minutes to midnight |
| Proof | Three pairs: buy / gift cards with prices | Two priced pairs, then four more in quick succession | Four new pairings, largest gift first |
| Music | 120 BPM darbuka and marimba, D minor → D major | 90 BPM piano and strings, F major | 120 BPM darbuka in Hijaz, with a clock tick on every beat |

**Hypothesis 01.** If the mechanic is understood in the first second and proven at three price points, launch-night viewers buy two pieces rather than one.

**Hypothesis 02.** On the second day, buyers of statement pieces respond to curated pairs, a precise rule and a dated deadline, producing larger orders.

**Hypothesis 03.** A visible deadline on the final day, with fresh pairings, converts warm audiences who have not yet bought.

## 4. Launch plan for a 52-hour offer

1. **Before 7:30 PM Friday.** Create the Shopify discount (see §6) and test it, then schedule film 01. Nothing should go live before the discount does.
2. **Friday, 7:30 PM → midnight.** Post film 01 as a Reel and a Story at 7:30 PM. Run it in one Sales campaign optimised for Purchase with Advantage+ campaign budget: prospecting in Egypt (25–60, broad) plus retargeting (site visitors in the last 30–60 days, Instagram and Facebook engagers in the last 90 days, add-to-carts). **Switch film 01 off at midnight,** because it says "tonight".
3. **Saturday.** Run film 02 in the same campaign and post it as a Reel. Read cost per purchase and items per order by ad set on Saturday evening, and move budget towards whichever ad set converts better.
4. **Sunday.** Run film 03 with the weekend's largest daily budget, weighted towards retargeting (add-to-carts, visitors, viewers of films 01 and 02). Film 02 may keep running; its deadline is dated. Post film 03 as a Reel and a Story in the morning and repeat the Story in the evening. **Stop every ad at midnight,** when the discount ends.
5. **Pause any other offer ads** for the weekend. The October films show full prices, and the Black Friday ads (ended 13 November) must not run.

A 52-hour window is too short for a statistically clean test; the plan is built to learn by Saturday evening and spend most on Sunday, when a real deadline lifts conversion.

## 5. Accuracy rules used in the films

- **Prices are exact.** Every price is the live storefront price recorded at 12:49 UTC on 20 November and re-recorded, unchanged, at 06:14 UTC on 21 November; nothing is rounded. The six pieces carried over from Black Friday have the same prices as on 6 November, so no gift value was inflated before the offer.
- **The free piece is computed, never chosen by hand.** `src/lib/bogo.ts` refuses to render a pair whose "free" piece costs more than the piece being bought.
- **"Today" and "tonight" appear only in the films for that day.** Film 01 says «الليلة» (Friday); film 03 says «النهارده» (Sunday); film 02 gives the date.
- **No promise about orders of three or more pieces.** Whether a four-piece order gets two free pieces depends on a Shopify setting (§6), so the films only promise one free piece for two.
- **No stock, demand or "bestseller" claims, and no authentication language.** Product names and attributes are quoted from each listing (see [ad-copy.md](ad-copy.md#claims-and-evidence)).

## 6. Risks to watch

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| The discount does not work as the films say | Create a Shopify **Buy X get Y** automatic discount: customer buys a minimum quantity of 1 item from all products; customer gets 1 item from all products at **Free**. Set it active from Fri 20 Nov, 7:30 PM to Sun 22 Nov, 11:59 PM. Before 7:30 PM, test carts with two pieces of different prices (for example the Sèvres pair and the cobalt centerpiece) and confirm the **lower-priced** piece becomes free and the higher-priced one stays at full price. |
| Orders of three or more pieces | Decide in Shopify's "Maximum number of uses per order" whether every second piece is free (no limit) or only one piece per order (limit 1). The films and copy work with either; customer service should know which one applies. |
| Margin | A close pair gives away up to half of the order's value, more than Black Friday's 40% top tier. Confirm the offer is profitable, especially on the close pairs the films promote. |
| A featured piece sells | Most pieces are one of a kind, and several appear in two films. Run `npm run check:availability -- 2026-11-bogo-weekend` on Saturday and Sunday mornings. If a piece sells, pause the affected film or swap the pair in its source and re-render (about 5 minutes per film). This happened on the first night: the Italian Murano vase sold, and film 02 was re-rendered with a new pair on Saturday morning. |
| A film runs on the wrong day | Film 01 says "tonight" and film 03 says "today". Schedule them exactly as in §4. |
| The offer is not visible on the site | Add a banner with "Buy 1, Get 1 Free · the lower-priced piece is free · until Sunday at midnight". Paid visitors should see the offer they clicked on. |

## 7. What to measure

In decision order:

1. **Purchases**, **cost per purchase** and **ROAS**, by film and by day.
2. **Items per order.** The direct test of the offer: most orders should contain two pieces or more.
3. **Average order value**, and how often the free piece is close in price to the paid one. This shows whether the close pairs drove behaviour.
4. **Discount application rate.** Every two-piece order should show the free item; any that does not points to a setup problem.
5. **Add-to-cart, checkout and link click-through rates.** These are diagnostic only.

These are hypotheses to test with live data, not predictions.

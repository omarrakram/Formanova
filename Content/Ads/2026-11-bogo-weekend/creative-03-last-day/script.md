## Copy rationale

- **Lead with the deadline.** On the last day, the most important new fact is that the offer ends tonight; «آخر يوم» and the clock say it before any product appears.
- **The biggest gift first.** The Sèvres candlesticks (EGP 14,399.99), free with the jardinère, are the most valuable free piece the store can give.
- **New pairings of familiar pieces.** Re-pairing pieces seen on Friday and Saturday shows that *any* two pieces qualify and gives returning viewers something new.
- **The same rule and reassurance as Friday.** «والأقل سعراً هدية», automatic at checkout, no code, delivery everywhere: no reason left to hesitate.

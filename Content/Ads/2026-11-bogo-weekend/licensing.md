## Product photography

- **Source.** All nine photographs were downloaded at full original resolution from formanovavintage.com and are stored unaltered in `Content/Production/assets/products/`. Their source URLs and checksums are in the manifest.
- **Treatment.** Photographs are never retouched, recoloured or regenerated. The films only crop, scale, move and mask them (cards and arches) and place type, labels and price panels beside them. No overlay covers the products themselves.
- **One edited listing image.** The Canton cachepot photograph (listing image 3) is the storefront's own edited listing image; its file name shows it was produced in Photoroom, a background-editing app. It is used exactly as Formanova published it.
- **Checks.** Every image used was inspected for watermarks; none carries one.
- **Authorisation.** Whoever runs the campaign should confirm they are authorised by Formanova to use its imagery and trademarks in paid media.

## Music and sound design

- **1+1, Tonight (01):** a 120 BPM darbuka-and-marimba groove on the Andalusian cadence (D minor, C, B♭, A). The gift card turns gold on a bell, each pair flips in on a downbeat, every free price is struck with a typed click and a bright accent, and the end card resolves to D major.
- **Two Pieces, One Price (02):** a 90 BPM piece for felt piano, strings and a string ostinato in F major (Fmaj9, Dm9, B♭maj7, Gm7, C, F). Each "FREE" lands on a bell and a glass ting.
- **Last Day (03):** a 120 BPM darbuka groove in the Hijaz mode on E, with a clock tick on every beat until the call to action, which lands on E major with bells.

Every sound is synthesised from mathematical models; **no samples, loops, sample libraries, AI music services or recordings were used**. The masters are `bogo-tonight-score.wav`, `bogo-pair-score.wav` and `bogo-last-day-score.wav` in `Content/Production/assets/audio/`: 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo, normalised to −14 LUFS with true peak at or below −1.2 dBTP, and regenerated byte-for-byte by `npm run score`. There are no third-party rights, royalties or content-ID risks. Ownership follows the agreement between Formanova and whoever commissioned the work.

## Copy rationale

- **"1+1" is the shortest possible statement of the offer** and is read the same way in Arabic and English; the line beneath it says it in words.
- **Right to left.** The piece you buy stands on the right, where an Arabic reader starts; the gift is second, on the left, in gold.
- **The rule is always on screen.** «والأقل سعراً هدية» answers "which one is free?" before checkout, the commonest source of Buy 1 Get 1 disappointment.
- **Three budgets.** EGP 14,399.99, 7,349.99 and 5,399.99 show that every viewer has a pair within reach.
- **The deadline is real.** Sunday at midnight is when the discount ends.

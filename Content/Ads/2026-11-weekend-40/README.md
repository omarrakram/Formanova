## Meta Ads

One Sales campaign, "40% Weekend · 27–29 Nov 2026", optimised for purchases on the "Forma new" pixel. It has one ad set, which runs from Friday 8 PM, when the discount starts, to Sunday 11:59 PM with a lifetime budget of EGP 3,000. Meta paces the spend across the weekend.

All seven ad films run for the whole window in their all-weekend wording ("until Sunday 11:59 PM"): Friday, Saturday A, "Already gone" (03, already true all weekend), Saturday C, Sunday A, Sunday B and Sunday C. The originals, with "tonight", "tomorrow" and "last day" lines, are for organic posts on their own day.

- **Placements:** Facebook and Instagram only (feed, Stories and Reels), with Advantage+ placements off. Each ad shows the film's 4:5 version in the feeds and the 9:16 master in Stories and Reels.
- **Audience:** Egypt, 18–65, Advantage+ audience, with Instagram and Facebook Page engagers (last 365 days) and antiques, collectibles, vases, Bohemian and luxury interests as suggestions.
- **Optimisation:** purchases, highest volume, 7-day click and 1-day engaged-view attribution.

Copy, headlines and links are in [ad-copy.md](ad-copy.md).

### All-weekend re-cuts

Six films state the end of the sale relative to their own day ("tonight", "until tomorrow, Sunday", "last day"), which would be false on the other days, so the ads use re-cuts instead. Each has a re-cut, `formanova-w40-<film>-wknd-15s.mp4` in the same folder, whose date lines say "until Sunday 11:59 PM" instead. The pieces, prices, pictures and music are unchanged; "Already gone" (03) already reads true all weekend.

| Re-cut | What changes |
|---|---|
| Friday | «من الليلة الساعة 8 لحد الأحد…» becomes «لحد الأحد 11:59 مساءً بس» |
| Saturday A | «لحد بكرة الأحد / بكرة بالليل / بكرة 11:59» become «الأحد…» |
| Saturday C | «لحد بكرة بالليل» becomes «لحد الأحد بالليل»; end card «بيخلص الأحد» |
| Sunday A | «آخر يوم» over the clock becomes «لحد الأحد بس»; «النهارده» becomes «الأحد»; «بكرة الأسعار بترجع» becomes «بعد الأحد الأسعار بترجع» |
| Sunday B | "LAST DAY … Ends tonight" becomes "THIS WEEKEND … Ends Sunday at 11:59 PM"; "Prices go back up on Monday." |
| Sunday C | «لحد النهارده» and «آخر يوم» become «لحد الأحد 11:59 مساءً» |

## Deliverables and QA

All 27 files (the eight films, six all-weekend re-cuts and 13 feed versions at 1080 × 1350) are H.264 High, yuv420p, BT.709, 1080 × 1920 unless 4:5, 30 fps, 450 frames and exactly 15.000 s, with AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo at −14.2 to −14.5 LUFS and true peaks of −1.4 dBTP or lower; `npm run verify` checks every declared property. None has black frames, silences or static segments; all copy, prices and products sit inside the Reels safe area.

Prices were recorded from the live store at 15:27 UTC on 27 November. A film can only show a piece as sold out if that check recorded it as sold out, and it cannot show a sold-out piece at 40% off (`src/lib/weekend40.ts`).

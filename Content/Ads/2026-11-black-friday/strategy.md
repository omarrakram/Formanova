# Black Friday 2026: strategy

**Sale:** Friday 6 November, 8 PM (Cairo) to Friday 13 November 2026
**Offer:** Buy 2 → 20% off · Buy 3 → 30% off · Buy 4 or more → 40% off. It is applied automatically at checkout to the whole order, every product is eligible, and there is no code.
**Goal:** the most purchases and the highest order values during a seven-day window, led by the most expensive pieces in stock.

The sale terms above were confirmed by the brand owner on 5 November 2026. Prices and availability come from the live storefront at **12:16 UTC on 6 November 2026**.

---

## 1. What this offer rewards, and what the ads must do

A quantity-tiered discount is an **order-value lever**: the discount only grows when the basket grows. The ads therefore have three jobs, in this order:

1. **Make the ladder instantly understood.** "The more pieces, the bigger the discount" has to land in about 5 seconds, with sound off.
2. **Make the top tier irresistible.** 40% is only reached with 4 or more items, so show what 40% means on real, expensive pieces.
3. **Give a real reason to act now.** The sale genuinely ends Friday 13 November. That is the only urgency used, and it is true.

Because the brand sells mostly one-of-a-kind pieces, the ads avoid "only one left" claims, even though they would often be true. Stock changes daily; see §6.

## 3. Two films, two hypotheses

| | 01 · The Ladder | 02 · The 40% Set |
|---|---|---|
| Language | Egyptian Arabic | English |
| Job | Explain the tiers; prove them on top pieces | Sell one premium four-piece order at 40% |
| Opening frame | Gold **40%** and "up to 40% off every piece" | "Choose four. Save 40%." over the four pieces |
| Proof | Cards fan out on the beat: 2 → 20%, 3 → 30%, 4+ → 40%; then struck-through prices | Each piece named with its price; then a receipt: EGP 83,900 → EGP 50,340 |
| Music | Original 120 BPM darbuka-and-marimba groove | Original 80 BPM piano and strings that build to the saving |
| Best for | Broad Egyptian audience; cold prospecting | Premium buyers; warm audiences (visitors, engagers) |

**Hypothesis 01, The Ladder.** If the quantity ladder is obvious within five seconds and then shown on the most prized pieces, more viewers will buy, and buy more than one item, than with a generic "up to 40%" message.

**Hypothesis 02, The 40% Set.** If buyers of statement pieces see a curated four-piece order and the exact saving, the ad will produce fewer but much larger orders (four or more items, about EGP 30,000 and up) at a profitable return on ad spend.

Both end with the deadline, the tiers, the automatic-discount reassurance and the URL. Each reads fully with the sound off.

## 4. Launch plan for a seven-day sale

A seven-day sale is too short for a slow, statistically clean A/B test, so the plan is built to learn fast:

1. **Schedule.** Start both ads at **20:00 Cairo time on Friday 6 November** (UTC+2), never before the discount is live. End them when the Shopify discount ends on Friday 13 November.
2. **Structure.** Use one Sales campaign optimised for Purchase with Advantage+ campaign budget, and put both ads in each ad set:
   - **Prospecting:** Egypt, ages 25–60, broad or Advantage+ audience.
   - **Retargeting:** website visitors in the last 30–60 days, Instagram and Facebook engagers in the last 90 days, and anyone who added to cart. Retargeting audiences usually convert best in short sales; give them 30–40% of the budget.
3. **First read at 48 hours.** Compare cost per purchase and ROAS by ad. If one ad has at least twice as many purchases at a lower cost per purchase, move about 70% of the spend to it. Otherwise keep both running; the platform will already be favouring the stronger one.
4. **Final 48 hours (from Wednesday 11 November).** Raise budgets. A real deadline lifts conversion in the last days of a sale; both films already state it.
5. **Pause the October ads while the sale runs.** They show full prices with no sale message, and three of their featured pieces are no longer on the store.

## 5. Accuracy rules used in the films

- **Prices** are the live storefront prices. Per-item sale prices are exact (for example, 40% off EGP 44,999.99 is EGP 26,999.99).
- **Receipt totals are shown in whole pounds.** Checkout may round a percentage discount per line or per order; the exact saving on the four-piece set is EGP 33,559.98 one way and EGP 33,560.00 the other. Whole pounds (EGP 83,900 − 33,560 = 50,340) are correct and consistent either way.
- **"4 items", not "4 pieces"**, on the receipt. The Sèvres candlesticks are sold as one listing (a pair), which counts as one item at checkout.
- **No stock, demand or "bestseller" claims, and no authentication language.** Product attributes are quoted from each listing (see [ad-copy.md](ad-copy.md)).
- **"Up to 40%"** («خصم لحد 40%») is used wherever the tier is not stated in the same line.

## 6. Risks to watch during the sale

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Reference prices: the prices shown as "before" were raised on 6 November, hours before the sale | Advertising a percentage off a price raised the same day can be treated as a misleading reference price under consumer-protection rules and Meta's advertising policies, and customers who saw earlier prices may notice. Consider restoring the pre-sale prices, or ask for versions without the struck-through prices. |
| A featured piece sells. Most stock is one of a kind, and the jardinère appears in both films. | Run `npm run check:availability -- 2026-11-black-friday` every morning. If a piece sells, swap it in the creative source and re-render (about 3 minutes per film), or pause the affected ad. |
| The discount does not apply as the ads promise | Before 8 PM on 6 November, place test carts with 2, 3 and 4 items (include the Sèvres pair) and confirm 20%, 30% and 40% are applied automatically to the whole order. |
| Margin at 40% on the most expensive pieces | Confirm the 40% tier is profitable on the featured pieces before launch. The ads deliberately push four-item orders. |
| The sale is not visible on the site | Add a Black Friday banner to the storefront with the same tiers and end date. Paid visitors should see the offer they clicked on. |

## 7. What to measure

In decision order:

1. **Cost per purchase** and **ROAS**.
2. **Average order value** and **items per order**. These are the direct test of the ladder offer.
3. **Share of orders with 2, 3 and 4+ items.** If most orders stop at 2 items, the 40% message needs more weight.
4. **Add-to-cart rate**, **checkout rate** and **link CTR**. These are diagnostic only.

Real performance will only be known from live data. These are hypotheses to test, not predictions.

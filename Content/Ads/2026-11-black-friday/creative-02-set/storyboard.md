## Visual system

The gallery arch from the October heritage film returns, so premium buyers recognise the brand. Here it works as a shop window for a four-piece order. One arch grows to present each piece; at the end the four arches line up above a cream receipt.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Palette | Espresso ground, ivory type (#F6EEE3), wordmark gold (#EFCE6C) with a gold gradient on "Save 40%", cream receipt (#FFFBF4) |
| Type | Playfair Display Italic (headlines and names), EB Garamond (labels, CTA), Anonymous Pro (prices and the receipt) |
| Rhythm | 80 BPM: a new piece every 1.5 s; receipt lines every half second |
| Safe area | Every arch, label, price and the receipt sit between y 270 and 1250 px ([QA sheet](../qa/safe-zone-check.jpg)) |

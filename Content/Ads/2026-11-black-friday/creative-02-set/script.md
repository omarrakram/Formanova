## Copy rationale

- **"Choose four. Save 40%."** names the top tier as a simple instruction. It is the most profitable behaviour for a tiered offer, and it fits in four words.
- **Real pieces, real prices, in sequence.** Each piece gets its own moment, with craftsmanship details quoted from its listing, so the set feels curated rather than discounted.
- **The receipt is the persuasion.** Seeing EGP 64,764 become EGP 38,858 makes the value tangible for buyers of statement pieces. Figures are whole pounds, so they are exact whichever way checkout rounds.
- **"4 items".** The Sèvres pair is one listing, so it counts as one item, and the receipt says so precisely.

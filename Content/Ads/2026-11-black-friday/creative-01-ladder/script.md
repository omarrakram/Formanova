## Copy rationale

- **Open on the maximum, honestly.** «خصم لحد 40%» ("up to") is the biggest number the offer allows, and it is true for every product.
- **Teach the mechanic with objects, not words.** One more card arrives as each tier lights up, so the viewer sees the basket grow with the discount.
- **Prove the top tier on the top pieces.** A struck-through EGP 32,409.99 becoming EGP 19,445.99 makes 40% concrete. The condition («مع 4 قطع أو أكتر») sits on every price panel.
- **Remove friction at the decision.** The end card states that the discount is automatic (no code), that delivery covers all of Egypt, and the real deadline.

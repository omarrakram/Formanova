## Product photography

- **Source.** All seven photographs were downloaded at full original resolution from formanovavintage.com and are stored unaltered in `Content/Production/assets/products/`. Their source URLs and checksums are in the manifest.
- **Treatment.** Photographs are never retouched, recoloured or regenerated. The films only crop, scale, move and mask them (cards and arches) and place type and price panels beside them. No overlay covers the products themselves.
- **Checks.** Every image used was inspected for watermarks; none carries one.
- **Authorisation.** Whoever runs the campaign should confirm they are authorised by Formanova to use its imagery and trademarks in paid media.

## Music and sound design

- **The Ladder (01):** a 120 BPM groove in A harmonic minor built on a synthesised darbuka playing maqsum, with marimba, bass and kick. Each tier change has its own rising hit, and each price lands with a typewriter strike. It resolves to A major on the end card.
- **The 40% Set (02):** an 80 BPM piece for felt piano, strings and a string ostinato. It moves from D minor through B♭, G minor and A, and resolves to D major on the saving.

Every sound is synthesised from mathematical models; **no samples, loops, sample libraries, AI music services or recordings were used**. The masters are `bf-ladder-score.wav` and `bf-set-score.wav` in `Content/Production/assets/audio/`: 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo, normalised to −14 LUFS with true peak at or below −1.2 dBTP, and regenerated byte-for-byte by `npm run score`. There are no third-party rights, royalties or content-ID risks. Ownership follows the agreement between Formanova and whoever commissioned the work.

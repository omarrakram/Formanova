## Copy rationale

- **Hook as a self-check question.** A viewer can't help answering it about their own home. The flip-book of unusual pieces is the visual answer.
- **«مش ديكور عادي.»** The concept in three words, in natural Egyptian Arabic.
- **Real prices on real pieces.** In-stock prices centre on EGP 1,850 (median), far below what many shoppers assume antiques cost. The typed tag makes the price feel like a shop label, not an ad overlay.
- **The brand's own Arabic vocabulary.** بلو بلا، شينواه، كلوازينه، فينتج، مياه الذهب come from Formanova's product descriptions, so the ad sounds like the brand.
- **No gendered imperatives in headlines.** Egyptian imperatives are gendered (اطلب / اطلبي). Headlines use statements; the button uses the standard e-commerce label «تسوّق الآن».
- **Delivery promise at the moment of decision.** The verified 3–5 working days, to every governorate.

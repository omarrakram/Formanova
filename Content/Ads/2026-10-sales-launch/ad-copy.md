### First performance test

Run as variant B of the A/B test in [strategy.md](strategy.md#5-test-plan). Expect a higher link CTR and a lower cost per landing-page view. If clicks are cheap but carts are empty, the landing page, not the ad, is the next thing to test.

## Not used, by design

- No scarcity or urgency claims ("only one left", countdowns), even though most pieces are single items.
- No discounts, reviews, ratings, "bestseller" or demand claims.
- No authentication claims ("certified", "original Sèvres"). Products are named exactly as listed.
- No emojis in the copy; the tone stays premium.

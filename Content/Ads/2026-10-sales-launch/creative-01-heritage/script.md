## Copy rationale

- **"Every home needs one piece with a past."** Relevant in the first second ("home"), intriguing ("a past") and achievable ("one piece"). It reframes vintage from "old and expensive" to "the thing that makes a room yours".
- **"Hand-painted. Hand-cut. Raised cloisonné."** Concrete craftsmanship, each verified against the product listing, gives the emotion substance.
- **"Chosen for character. Not for trends."** The brand's own positioning ("timelessness over trends", "selected with intention") in six words.
- **"Bringing the past into the future."** The brand's tagline from its Instagram bio, kept verbatim for brand consistency.
- **"Find your piece with a past."** The call to action closes the loop opened by the hook.
